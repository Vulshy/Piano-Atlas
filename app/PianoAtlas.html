About Piano Atlas

Piano Atlas is a compact practice reference for piano scales, chords, arpeggios, fingerings, chord progressions, and the circle of fifths.

  • Play Plays the selected scale, chord, arpeggio, or circle reference.
  • Pattern Plays the notes as a moving pattern, useful for hearing the shape.
  • Favorites Saves the current item in the sidebar so you can return to it quickly.
